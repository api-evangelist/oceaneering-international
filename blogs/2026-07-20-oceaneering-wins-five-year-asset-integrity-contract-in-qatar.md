Houston – Oceaneering International, Inc. (Oceaneering) announced that its Integrity Management and Digital Solutions (IMDS) segment has been awarded a five‑year inspection and asset integrity services contract by a Qatar-based oil company. The contract, which includes two optional one-year extensions, is expected to contribute meaningful revenue over its initial term.
